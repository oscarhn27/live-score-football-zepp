# Frequently asked questions

### I can't find my league in the watch selector

The watch only shows leagues you have **enabled** from your phone. Go to
the app's settings in Zepp and enable it — see the [installation
guide](INSTALLATION.md#choosing-your-leagues).

### A live match isn't updating instantly

Data has a small caching margin (a few minutes) to keep the service stable
and fast. The score and events refresh automatically every time you enter
or return to the match screen.

### I don't see any events (goals, cards...) for a match

Some matches, especially from smaller competitions, may not have detailed
events available yet, or the data provider hasn't published them yet.

### The app doesn't load anything / the screen is empty

Check that:

- The watch is connected to the Zepp app on your phone.
- Your phone has an internet connection.
- You have at least one league enabled in the app's settings.

If the problem persists, open an [issue](/issues) telling us your watch
model and what you see on screen.

### Do I need an account or to sign up to use the app?

No. The app doesn't ask for any registration or personal data.

### Can I use the app without my phone nearby?

No. The watch app has no direct internet access: it always needs the
paired phone, with a connection, to request the data.

### Where does the match data come from?

From [live-score-api.com](https://live-score-api.com/), a third-party
football results provider.
