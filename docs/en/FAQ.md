# Frequently asked questions

<details>
<summary>I can't find my league in the watch selector</summary>

The watch only shows leagues you have **enabled** from your phone. Go to
the app's settings in Zepp and enable it — see the [installation
guide](INSTALLATION.md#choosing-your-leagues).

</details>

<details>
<summary>A live match isn't updating instantly</summary>

Data has a small caching margin (a few minutes) to keep the service stable
and fast. The score and events refresh automatically every time you enter
or return to the match screen.

</details>

<details>
<summary>I don't see any events (goals, cards...) for a match</summary>

Some matches, especially from smaller competitions, may not have detailed
events available yet, or the data provider hasn't published them yet.

</details>

<details>
<summary>The app doesn't load anything / the screen is empty</summary>

Check that:

- The watch is connected to the Zepp app on your phone.
- Your phone has an internet connection.
- You have at least one league enabled in the app's settings.

If the problem persists, open an [issue](/issues) telling us your watch
model and what you see on screen.

</details>

<details>
<summary>Do I need an account or to sign up to use the app?</summary>

No. The app doesn't ask for any registration or personal data.

</details>

<details>
<summary>Can I use the app without my phone nearby?</summary>

No. The watch app has no direct internet access: it always needs the
paired phone, with a connection, to request the data.

</details>

<details>
<summary>Is the app free?</summary>

Not quite: there's a one-week free trial, after which the app costs $1.99.

</details>

<details>
<summary>Why isn't it free?</summary>

That price covers the services the app depends on, which aren't free
themselves: the live-score-api.com data provider and the backend
infrastructure that talks to it. In exchange, unlike many mobile apps or
websites, Football live score has no ads, no betting odds shown anywhere, and
your usage data is never sold to third parties.

</details>

<details>
<summary>Where does the match data come from?</summary>

From [live-score-api.com](https://live-score-api.com/), a third-party
football results provider.

</details>
