# Privacy

Football Live is designed to ask for as little data as possible.

## What we DON'T collect

- We don't require registration or a user account.
- We don't access your contacts, precise location, or any other data from
  your phone or watch beyond what's strictly needed to show matches.
- We don't share or sell data to third parties.

## What we do collect

To understand which leagues and features are used the most (and improve
the app), we anonymously store:

- What kind of request was made (e.g. live matches, upcoming matches, a
  match's events) and for which competition.
- An approximate country, inferred automatically from your internet
  connection (not from the device's location data).
- The date and time of the request.

This data **includes no personal identifier**: we don't store your name,
an account, or a device identifier that would let us recognize you across
visits. It's only used in aggregate, to know things like "how many
requests were made this week for LaLiga" — never to track a specific
person's activity.

## How long is it kept?

These records are automatically deleted after some time (by default, one
year).

## Contact

If you have questions about privacy, open an [issue](/issues) on this
repository.
